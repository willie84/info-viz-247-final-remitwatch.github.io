The Scale of the Problem

Amina's missing money isn't a glitch.
It's a global system.

Millions of families lose billions to hidden margins and high fees. Let's let the World Bank data show exactly where the money goes.

01 / The Providers

Banks charge 3× more than mobile apps.

Just like SendFree hid its fee in a bad exchange rate, traditional commercial banks average an 8.3% global cost. Mobile operators sit near 3.1%.

02 / Amina's Route

Brand names mean higher fees.

We made up our apps on page one, but here is the real UK → Kenya data. Transparent apps cost ~1%. Legacy giants like Western Union sit over 8%.

03 / The Global Toll

Sub-Saharan Africa pays the highest tax on earth.

The UN target is 3%. Sub-Saharan Africa averages 11.5%. This system drains capital directly from the communities that need it most.

04 / Global Inequality

Where you send from dictates the price.

Amina's UK-to-Kenya route is competitive. But look at other countries sending into Kenya. A fair remittance system requires global, not just regional, transparency.

Data Source: World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide (RPW)
